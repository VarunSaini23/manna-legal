Manna Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026. Last updated: 27 September 2026.

Manna is a Bible reading app. This page describes what data the app uses. It is written in plain language on purpose.

No accounts

Manna does not create user accounts. It does not collect your name, email address, or location.

What stays on your device

Saved verses, notes, settings (translation, text size, theme, reminder time, daily goal), and a local usage event log are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Purchases

If you subscribe, Apple or Google handle the payment. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase/subscription status. It does not receive your name or email from Manna.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on the device as local notifications. There is no push-notification server.

Study content updates

If we later turn on over-the-air study updates, the app will download files from a static host. That host will see an IP address the same way any website does. This feature is off in the current version.

What we do not do

No ads. No tracking across other apps. No selling of data. Manna is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

A dedicated support email will be published here before the App Store listing is submitted. Until then you can open a request at github.com/VarunSaini23/manna-legal/issues.
