Scroll Prayer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Scroll Prayer is a Bible reading app. This page describes what data the app uses. It is written in plain language on purpose.

No accounts

Scroll Prayer does not create user accounts. It does not ask for your name, email address, or precise location.

What stays on your device

Saved verses, notes, settings (translation, text size, theme, reminder time, daily goal), and a local usage event log are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Purchases

If you subscribe, Apple or Google handle the payment. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase/subscription status. It does not receive your name or email from Scroll Prayer.

Usage analytics

The app sends a short list of usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see whether people get through the introduction, open a study, and how long a visit lasts. Those events are the introduction screen number, introduction finished, a study opened (only its depth, and whether it was the introduction example), a study closed, the free-study limit reached, and the subscription screen opened. Google also records that the app was opened and how long it stayed on screen. We do not send your name, email, notes, search text, or which verse, book, or chapter you read. The id is an anonymous one created on the phone. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on the device as local notifications. There is no push-notification server.

Study content updates

If we later turn on over-the-air study updates, the app will download files from a static host. That host will see an IP address the same way any website does. This feature is off in the current version.

What we do not do

No ads. No tracking across other apps. No selling of data. Scroll Prayer is not directed at children under 13.
