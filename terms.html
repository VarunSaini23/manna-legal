The product

Manna lets you read the Bible as a full-screen feed and open optional study notes. Scripture in the app is free. Study notes beyond a small free allowance require a subscription (“Manna Premium”).

Study notes are a reference aid. They are not pastoral care, legal advice, or a substitute for your own reading or a faith community.

Subscriptions

  • Monthly: USD $4.99 per month (no free trial).
  • Annual: USD $29.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers where Apple or Google offer it.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You manage or cancel in App Store or Google Play account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by Manna.

Prices may vary by storefront. The store sheet on your device is the source of truth.
