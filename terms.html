The product

Scroll Prayer lets you read the Bible as a full-screen feed and open optional study notes. Scripture in the app is free. Study notes beyond a small free allowance require a subscription (“Premium”).

Study notes are a reference aid. They are not pastoral care, legal advice, or a substitute for your own reading or a faith community.
